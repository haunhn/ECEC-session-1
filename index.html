Guided Speaking Practice

Follow the step-by-step flow (➔) to build your answer, then click the button to reveal the sample responses.

Level: A1 – A2 Question #1

Can you introduce yourself and tell us about your major?

Speaking Flow Guide:

Greeting & Name ➔ University Year ➔ Your Major ➔ One reason why you enjoy it.

Standard (A1–B1): "Hi everyone! My name is Minh. I am a freshman at the University of Science and Education. I major in English Education. I chose this major because I love working with students and sharing knowledge."

B2 Upgrade: "Hello everyone, I’m Minh. Currently, I'm a sophomore pursuing a degree in Primary Education. What really drew me to this field is my passion for inspiring children. Today, I want to step out of my comfort zone and make new friends at ECEC!"

Level: A2 – B1 Question #2

Do you live in a dormitory or rent an apartment outside? Which one do you prefer?

Speaking Flow Guide:

Current place ➔ 1 Main Advantage (cost / freedom) ➔ 1 Small Disadvantage (noise / curfew).

Standard (A1–B1): "Right now, I rent a shared apartment near my campus with two roommates. I prefer renting a room because I have more freedom and can cook my own meals, even though it is more expensive than a dormitory."

B2 Upgrade: "I'm currently staying in the university dormitory. Honestly, I prefer it because it reduces my living expenses significantly, although it can get a bit noisy when I need to hit the books."

Level: B1 Question #3

How is university life different from high school for you?

Speaking Flow Guide:

Flexible schedule ➔ Self-study & group projects ➔ Joining clubs & part-time jobs.

Standard (B1): "For me, university life gives me much more independence than high school. Professors don't check our homework every single day, so self-study is super important. Also, I have more free time to join extracurricular activities like ECEC and work a part-time job."

🔥 Level: B2 Challenge Question #4

How do you balance your studies, part-time job, and social life?

Speaking Flow Guide:

Admit the challenge ➔ Time management tool (planner / calendar) ➔ Prioritize deadlines.

B2 Sample: "To be honest, it can be tough to strike a balance between academics and my part-time job. To stay on track, I use a digital calendar to plan my week in advance so I can always meet the deadline without having to pull an all-nighter before exams."

About Our Community

ECEC ENGLISH CLUB

ECEC is the official English Club under the University of Science and Education (UED). We are a vibrant community dedicated to university students who share a burning passion for learning and developing their English proficiency.

By joining ECEC, you not only step into a supportive environment to sharpen your English and essential soft skills through dynamic club activities, but you also get the chance to meet wonderful new friends and find your true soulmates during your unforgettable university years.
